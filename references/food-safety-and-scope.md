# Food safety and scope

- Do not taste raw cake batter. CDC states uncooked flour can carry pathogens including *E. coli* and *Salmonella*, and raw eggs can carry *Salmonella*. Baking/cooking according to reliable instructions kills germs; ordinary grinding/bleaching does not make flour safe to eat raw.
- Wash hands, bowls, utensils, counters, and other surfaces after handling raw flour, eggs, or dough. Keep raw ingredients away from ready-to-eat food. Follow product labels and current local food-safety guidance for storage and cooking.
- Do not try to rescue spoiled, recalled, or questionably stored food by baking. Discard recalled products; do not claim an unknown temperature/time history is safe.
- For allergies, check exact product labels and consider cross-contact. Do not guarantee allergen safety: ingredients can contain undeclared traces or be contaminated during handling. The project is not a substitute for professional medical or food-safety advice.
- “Edible ingredient” is a necessary constraint. Refuse non-food materials, toxic substances, and unsafe requests. If a request concerns specialized diets (including vegan, gluten-free, low-sugar, or allergy-safe), explicitly state that this package does not provide comprehensive tested support.

This is a concise reminder, not an exhaustive safety protocol. Sources and exact scope: [`../sources/source-notes.md`](../sources/source-notes.md).
