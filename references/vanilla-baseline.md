# Practical vanilla-cake comparison baseline

## Selected anchor

Use a conventional creamed vanilla butter cake as a comparison anchor: flour, sugar, butter, eggs, milk, baking powder, salt, and vanilla, mixed by a creaming method and baked in a conventional pan. King Arthur Baking's [Lemon Bliss Cake](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/lemon-bliss-cake-recipe) is an accessible example of a butter-cake creaming method; its lemon flavor makes it illustrative rather than the literal vanilla baseline. Their [Golden Vanilla Cake](https://www.kingarthurbaking.com/recipes/golden-vanilla-cake-recipe) is a vanilla cake example using a paste/reverse-creaming method. Do not present either as the uniquely ideal or universally best formula.

## Comparison dimensions

- **Flavor:** pleasant, balanced vanilla/sweet/butter profile relative to this anchor.
- **Structure:** ability to set, hold shape, and slice as a cake.
- **Texture:** tenderness, moisture, and crumb relative to a conventional layer/bundt-style butter cake.
- **Leavening:** plausible lift and crumb openness relative to a cake using a suitable leavener and/or its intended air-incorporation method.

The package's 0–5 rubric is an explicit project heuristic; no controlled bake trials validate these scores. Scores estimate ingredient/formula alignment only, not bake success or user skill. See the [cake formulator skill](../.agents/skills/cake-formulator/SKILL.md).
